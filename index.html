From fruit to fizz

How a can of Sprunk gets made.

  1. 1

    Press

    Lemons and limes are pressed within a day of picking, so the juice stays bright.

  2. 2

    Blend

    The juice is mixed with cane sugar, citric acid and filtered water.

  3. 3

    Chill

    The blend is cooled to 1 °C, because cold liquid holds more CO2.

  4. 4

    Carbonate and seal

    We charge it to 3.6 volumes of CO2 and seal the can in under a second.
